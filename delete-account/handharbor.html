In this test version of Handharbor, accounts exist only inside the app while it is open. Nothing is sent to a server or saved on your phone.

To delete your account: close the app. Your account, and anything you entered, is gone, and the app starts again from the sample data next time. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

What is deleted

  • your name and email as you typed them;
  • any broker application, project request or review notes you made.

What is kept

Nothing. We hold no copy of anything you enter in this version.
