Handharbor broker accounts can be deleted in the app or by email. Clients don't have accounts, but they can ask us to delete anything they sent.

Delete your broker account in the app

  1. Open Handharbor and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away, and you come off the register.

Delete it by email

If you can't sign in, email victorfolorunsoofficial@gmail.com from the address you applied with, with the subject "Delete my Handharbor account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in (email and password);
  • your application and your public listing;
  • your face photo and intro video.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Messages clients sent you, reports about you and clients' video reviews of your work are kept for up to 12 months in case of disputes, then deleted. Email us to have them deleted sooner.
  • Your verification number is not reused.

If you are a client

Email us with the broker's name and the email you used, and say whether it was a message, a report or a video review. We delete it within 30 days and take any published video down.
