ContactSync is an app made by wonderperk ("we"). It connects you with the kinds of people you choose (your "circles") and adds you to each other's phone contacts. This policy explains what we do with information about you.

The short version: the people you sync with get your phone number, username, photo, bio, tags, your state and what you're here to do. That is the service, and it always works both ways. Your phone's address book never leaves your phone. We never sell your data or use it for ads.

What we collect

  • Your phone number: your account and how you sign in. We confirm it by sending a code to your WhatsApp. It is shared with the people you sync with.
  • Your profile: username, and optionally a photo and a short bio. Shared with the people you sync with.
  • Your tags: used to match you. The people you sync with can see your tags.
  • About you (all optional): your gender, birth year, state, and whether you're here to sell, buy or network. Other members' circles use these to match you. Your birth year is never shown to anyone. Your gender and age are shown to the people you sync with only if you switch that on. Your state and what you're here to do are shown to the people you sync with. We don't ask about religion, ethnicity or health. You must be 18 or over, and we refuse a birth year that says otherwise.
  • Your circles: the filters you set for who you sync with (for example tags, gender, age range, states). They are private to you. When we show how many people a circle would match, we never show a number under 5.
  • Your connections: who you are synced with, and who you have kept, blocked, reported or said hi to. Reports are never shown to the person reported.
  • Your access: when your free month ends and, once subscriptions open, your subscription status. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Invites: your invite code, who invited you (if anyone), and the free months earned when people you invited subscribe. The person who invited you sees only how many people joined and subscribed, never who you are.
  • One-time benefits: so the free month, promo codes and invite codes can't be claimed again by deleting and recreating an account, we keep a scrambled one-way code made from your phone number (not the number itself) with a note of which of these it has used.
  • Sign-in requests: the phone number and time each sign-in code was requested, to stop abuse.

Reminder settings are stored on your phone only.

Our web page (the ContactSync tester page) counts visits and taps on its buttons, with only the kind of device (Android, iPhone or other), whether it was opened inside TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, and which link it came from. No cookies, IP addresses or anything that identifies you are stored.

Your phone's address book

ContactSync needs access to your contacts to add the people you sync with. It reads your address book on your phone only, to avoid adding people you already have. Your address book is never uploaded, stored by us or shared.

ContactSync only removes contacts it added itself, and only if you haven't edited them. A contact you edit or choose to keep is yours for good.

Something important about phone numbers

Once your number is on someone's phone, their phone may copy it to their own backups (for example Google Contacts or iCloud) and to other apps. When a connection ends, ContactSync removes the contact it added, but it can't reach those copies. Only sync if you're comfortable with that.

Who we share with

  • The people you sync with see your phone number, username, photo, bio and tags, your state and what you're here to do, and your gender and age if you chose to show them. They don't see your circles or your birth year.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us: Supabase (hosting and database, in the EU, Ireland) and Sendchamp (delivers sign-in codes on WhatsApp; the message may show "Flutterwave" as the sender). Google Play handles subscriptions.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We never sell your data, and we don't share it for advertising. The app has no ads and no advertising identifiers.

Keeping the community safe

You can block or report anyone. When three different members have each blocked and reported the same number, that number is banned and can't sign up again. We keep banned numbers for that reason.

How long we keep data

  • Your account, profile, tags, About you details, circles, connections, blocks and reports: until you delete your account. You can clear any About you detail at any time in the app.
  • If your access ends, your connections stay for 7 days, then the contacts ContactSync added are removed. Your profile stays so you can come back, until you delete your account.
  • Banned numbers: kept so the same number can't sign up again.
  • The scrambled one-way code of your number and which one-time benefits it used: kept after you delete your account, for up to 2 years after its last use, to prevent abuse of the free month and codes.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile > Delete account. This deletes your profile, photo, tags, About you details, circles, connections, blocks and reports straight away, and your number is removed from other members' phones on their next sync (unless they edited or kept your contact). Can't open the app? See how to delete your account.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, withdraw your consent and get a copy of your data. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Age

ContactSync is for people 18 and over. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every action is checked on our servers, not only in the app, and access to the database is restricted.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before a material change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest change.
