LINKING is a board game made by wonderperk ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

The short version: your game data stays on your phone and we never receive it. The game shows optional video ads from Google, and Google's ad service collects some device data to do that (see Ads).

What the game stores on your phone

  • your coins, the skins you own and the one you use;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, text size, reduce motion);
  • your match record (games played, won, drawn and lost);
  • an unfinished match, so you can continue it.

This stays on your phone. We have no servers and never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads

LINKING shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (to earn coins). There are no ads that appear by themselves. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app start time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and used under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Checking the time

Free coins top up every hour. So that changing your phone's clock doesn't refill them, the game asks a large public website (Google, or Cloudflare if Google can't be reached) for the current time: it reads only the date and time from a tiny empty reply. Like any website visit this shows them your IP address; the game sends nothing else and never contacts us.

Link Up (playing a friend nearby)

Link Up connects two phones directly over Bluetooth, with no internet and no server in between. To do this the game asks for Bluetooth ("Nearby devices") permission. It only shares what the match needs (the board, the moves, and the pawn skin each player uses) with the phone you connect to. It does not use Bluetooth to find your location, and it does not look for or connect to anything else.

Purchases

Coin packs, when offered, are bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded, so it can add the coins.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We never receive, sell or share your game data.

Children

LINKING is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Your game data is only on your phone: uninstalling LINKING deletes it. For ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or see Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new (for example online play or cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest change.
