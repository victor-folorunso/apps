Odd One Out: Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Odd One Out is a game made by wonderperk ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

The short version: your game data stays on your phone and we never receive it. The game shows optional video ads from Google, and Google's ad service collects some device data to do that (see Ads). There is no account and no server of ours.

What stays on your phone

  • your progress: levels, stars, coins, hints, streak and daily goals;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration and daily reminder time.

None of it is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Ads

Odd One Out shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (to earn coins, or an extra heart when you run out). There are no ads that appear by themselves. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app start time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and used under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices. On iPhone the game does not ask to track you across other apps, so ads there don't use your device's advertising identifier.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the game schedules a notification on your phone at the time you choose. It is scheduled locally by your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Coins and hints can be earned by playing. Coin packs, when offered, are bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded, so it can add the coins.

Children

The game is meant for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children. We collect nothing from anyone ourselves; ads are served by Google as described above.

Permissions

The game asks only for permission to show notifications, and only if you turn on reminders. It asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. If the game starts collecting anything new, this policy and the app's Data safety section on Google Play are updated first.
