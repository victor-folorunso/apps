Odd One Out: Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Odd One Out is a game made by wonderperk ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

The short version: Odd One Out collects nothing. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • your progress: levels, stars, coins, hints, streak and daily goals;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration and daily reminder time.

None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the game schedules a notification on your phone at the time you choose. It is scheduled locally by your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Coins and hints are earned by playing. There are no real-money purchases in this version.

Children

The game is meant for ages 13 and up and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Permissions

The game asks only for permission to show notifications, and only if you turn on reminders. It asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Any version of the game that started collecting data would need a new policy and a new Data safety declaration on Google Play, which you would see before updating.
