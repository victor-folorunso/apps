How ContactSync works

You build circles that describe who you want to meet: people with your tags, buyers or sellers, a country or region, an age range or a gender. When someone fits one of your circles and you fit one of theirs, you are added to each other's phone contacts. It always works both ways: you only get someone's number when they get yours.

Common questions

Nobody has synced with me yet. Check that ContactSync can access your contacts (your phone's Settings > ContactSync > Contacts), that your plan or free month is active (Profile > Subscription), and that your circles aren't too narrow. The circle screen shows how many people a circle matches before you save it.

Someone is bothering me. Open their contact in ContactSync and tap Block or Report. A blocked member is removed from your phone at once and can't sync with you again. Reports about anyone who may be under 18 or a risk to children are acted on straight away.

I don't want someone in my contacts any more. Open them in ContactSync and remove them, or leave the circle that matched you. Contacts that ContactSync added are removed by ContactSync; contacts you saved yourself are never touched.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription? Subscriptions are handled by your phone's app store. Open your app store's subscription settings, choose ContactSync and cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. After it ends, new people stop being added, and the contacts ContactSync added are removed 7 days later.

I changed phones or reinstalled. Sign in with the same phone number, then tap Profile > Subscription > Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile > Delete account. You can also ask us to delete it.
