ContactSync: Delete your account

In the app (fastest): open ContactSync, go to Profile > Delete account and confirm.

Can't open the app? Email victorfolorunsoofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my ContactSync account" and the phone number you signed up with. We'll confirm the number belongs to you (by a code sent to your WhatsApp) and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • your profile: username, photo and bio;
  • your phone number on your account, your tags and sync choice;
  • your connections, and the blocks, reports and "say hi" records you made;
  • your free-month and subscription status.

Your number is removed from other members' phones on their next sync, unless they edited or kept your contact (then it's theirs).

What is kept

  • If your number was banned after reports, the ban is kept so the number can't sign up again.
  • Records of sign-in code requests (phone number and time), kept to stop abuse.

An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
