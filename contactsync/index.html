ContactSync

Meet people in your niche.

Pick tags that describe you. ContactSync matches you with people who share them and adds you to each other's phone contacts, always both ways. Your address book never leaves your phone. 18+.

ContactSync is in testing on Google Play. Two taps on your Android phone, with the same Google account as your Play Store:

1. Join the testers (once; it covers every wonderperk app). Join testers
2. Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play. Get ContactSync

Just joined? Step 2 can take a few minutes to recognise you. Please keep the app installed for two weeks: Google counts testers before the public release.
