ContactSync: Child safety standards

Last updated 9 October 2026

ContactSync is for adults only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexualisation of minors.

Our standards

  • ContactSync is for people 18 and over. Everyone confirms they are 18 or older when they sign up.
  • It is forbidden to use ContactSync to share, request or link to sexual content involving minors, to contact a minor for sexual purposes, or to help anyone else do so.
  • Any profile photo, username, bio or tag that sexualises minors is removed, and the account is banned.
  • Accounts that belong to people under 18 are removed.

Reporting in the app

On any member's page, tap … > Report and choose Child safety concern or Under 18. Such a report acts immediately: the member is taken out of matching and removed from everyone's contacts while we review it. The member is never told who reported them.

You can also email victorfolorunsoofficial@gmail.com with the subject "ContactSync child safety report".

What we do with a report

  • We review child-safety reports as a priority, within 24 hours.
  • Where there is CSAE or CSAM, we remove the content, ban the account and phone number permanently, and preserve the information the law requires.
  • We report CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities: in Nigeria, the National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons (NAPTIP) and the Nigeria Police Force; and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other national authorities where they apply.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal channels.

Compliance

ContactSync complies with applicable child-safety laws, including Nigeria's Child Rights Act 2003, the Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015 and the Trafficking in Persons (Prohibition) Enforcement and Administration Act 2015, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
