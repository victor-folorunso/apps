Handharbor: Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Handharbor is an app made by wonderperk ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who sees it, and how to have it deleted.

The short version: Handharbor lists project brokers we have checked by hand. Brokers give us their name, email, face photo, intro video and freelance profile links so we can check them, and their listing is public. Clients can message a broker, report one or leave a video review; what they send goes to that broker or to our reviewers. There are no ads and no analytics, we never sell data, and we never handle project money.

Who runs it

wonderperk runs Handharbor and decides how this information is used. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage and Authentication), on Google's servers. Google processes it for us and may store it outside your country.

If you are a broker

When you apply, we collect:

  • account details: your name, email and a password (the password goes to Firebase Authentication and we never see it);
  • your listing: headline, location, the kinds of work you take and your usual project size;
  • a photo of your face and a short intro video, which you choose from your phone;
  • links to your Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn or similar profiles, and what a reviewer reads there: jobs completed, earnings and client reviews.

We use this to check that you are a real person who has delivered real projects, and then to list you. Once you are approved, your name, photo, intro video, headline, location, profile links, checked reviews and verification number are public in the app and can be seen by anyone. Your email is not shown on your listing.

Before approval a reviewer also checks your ID on a video call. We arrange that call by email. We do not record the call and we do not keep a copy of your ID: the reviewer notes only that the check passed and when.

Listings are rechecked every 30 days. When you renew, a reviewer looks at your profiles again.

If you are a client

You don't need an account. The app gives your phone an anonymous sign-in so that only you can send messages from it. It has no name or email attached.

  • Messaging a broker: we store your name, email, message and budget if you give one, and the broker you chose can read it, so they can reply to you by email.
  • Reporting a broker: we store the reason, your details and your email if you give one. Only our reviewers see reports. The broker is not told who reported them.
  • Leaving a video review: we store your video, your name, your role if you give one, a one-line summary, the project, where you hired the broker and your name on that platform, and your email. Once a reviewer has found the job, your video, name, role and summary are shown publicly on the broker's profile. Your email and platform name are never shown and are never given to the broker; reviewers use them only to check the job.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't take payments. You hire and pay brokers on their own platform, under that platform's terms.

Who can see what

  • Anyone: approved broker listings and published video reviews.
  • A broker: their own application, and the messages clients send them.
  • Our reviewers: applications, reports, video reviews waiting to be checked and the private details that come with them.
  • Google Firebase, which stores the data for us.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud.

How long we keep it

  • Broker accounts, applications and listings: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them straight away.
  • Client messages, reports and video reviews: for as long as the broker is listed, and for up to 12 months afterwards in case of disputes. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Rejected applications and rejected reviews: up to 12 months, so the same thing isn't submitted again, unless you ask us to delete them.

Deleting your information

Brokers can delete their account in the app, under Account → Delete my account. To delete a message, report or video review you sent, or anything else, email us. See delete your account for the full steps.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules on our database let each person read only what this policy says they can see.

Children

Handharbor is for adults hiring for projects and for professional brokers. It is not aimed at children, and you must be 18 or older to apply as a broker.

Permissions

Handharbor asks for access to your photos only when you choose a photo or video to upload. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date above before the change happens.
