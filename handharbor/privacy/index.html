Handharbor: Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Handharbor is an app made by wonderperk ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

The short version: this test version of Handharbor collects nothing. It runs on sample data inside the app. There is no server, no analytics and no ads, and nothing you type leaves your phone.

What the test version does

Handharbor connects clients with vetted project brokers. In this test version every broker, project and review is sample data built into the app, so you can try the client, broker and reviewer sides without anything being real.

When you sign in, sign up, apply as a broker or send a project request, what you type is held in the app's memory only. It is not sent anywhere and not saved to your phone's storage: closing the app clears it, and the app starts again from the sample data.

Accounts

Accounts in this version are pretend accounts for trying the app. No password is checked or stored, and no account exists anywhere outside the app while it is open. See delete your account.

Identity checks

The real service will check brokers' identity on a video call before they are listed. This test version does not do that: the review screens show how vetting will work, using sample applicants. No ID, photo or call is collected.

Children

Handharbor is a service for businesses and adults hiring for projects, and is not aimed at children. It collects nothing from anyone.

Permissions

Handharbor requests no runtime permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

Changes

When Handharbor connects to a real service, with real accounts and broker checks, this policy will be rewritten to say exactly what is collected and why, and the date above will change. Google Play's Data safety section will be updated at the same time, and you will see both before updating.
