Handharbor Concept project

One broker. Your whole project.

A directory of digital project brokers: people who take on a client's whole project, put the team together and deliver it. Every broker on the register was checked by a real person before they appeared, and again every 30 days. Clients hire them where their record lives, on Upwork, Fiverr or LinkedIn, so Handharbor never touches project money.

Recorded from the app on sample data: the welcome screen, the register, a broker's profile and credential, and the review queue.

How it works

  • Brokers apply with a photo of their face, a short intro video and links to their freelance profiles.
  • A reviewer opens each profile to confirm the jobs and reviews are really theirs, then sees their government ID on a video call.
  • Passing brokers get a numbered credential with an engraving drawn from their verification number, and a 30-day window that runs down on the card.
  • Clients message a broker from the app, report one in two taps, or record a video review, which goes up only after a reviewer finds the real job.
  • Built with React Native (Expo) and Firebase: Auth, Firestore, Storage and access rules that let each account read only what it should.

Status

Handharbor was built in 2026 and parked before public release. The app, the vetting workflow and the backend were finished; paid broker subscriptions were in progress when the project was shelved.
